Data export guide

How to export your shipping data

Shopify

Settings → Shipping and delivery → Manage rates

Or: Orders → Export → All orders → CSV

We need: order date, shipping method, carrier, tracking, destination zip, weight, shipping cost.

ShipStation

Shipments tab → Filter by date range → Export CSV

We need: ship date, carrier, service, zone, weight, dimensions, cost, surcharges.

UPS / FedEx portal

Billing Center → Download invoices → CSV format

We need: invoice detail (not summary). Each shipment as a separate row with tracking, zone, weight, and all charges.

Using a different platform? Email us at support@bizanalytic.com and we'll send you specific export instructions.
