{# ================================================================ #} {# THE PROBLEM #} {# ================================================================ #}
The problem

Most SMB shippers overpay on freight by 8-15%

A shipper spending $200K/month on freight is leaking $16K-$30K/month without knowing it. The reasons are always the same: rates negotiated annually with gut feel instead of data, accessorial charges that accumulate unnoticed, carrier invoices accepted without lane-level benchmarking, and no visibility into which lanes are profitable and which are bleeding money.

Enterprise shippers solve this with $100K+/year audit firms like Chainalytics. But if your freight spend is $50K-$500K/month, no affordable audit option exists. Your data already sits in TMS exports or carrier invoices as CSVs. Nobody is turning those CSVs into dollar-specific savings reports. Until now.

{# ================================================================ #} {# WHAT YOU GET #} {# ================================================================ #}
What you get

A 15-page Freight Spend Intelligence Report

Not a dashboard. Not a spreadsheet. A branded, executive-grade analysis you can hand to your CFO on Monday morning.

01

Lane-level cost benchmarks

Every lane in your network, ranked by cost per mile. We show you which lanes are above fleet median and by how much — with dollar amounts, not percentages.

02

Carrier rate anomalies

Statistical outlier detection (IQR) flags individual invoices priced significantly above the lane median. We list each anomalous shipment with the exact overpayment amount.

03

Accessorial charge patterns

Detention, liftgate, residential delivery, fuel surcharges — we break down where your accessorials concentrate and which carriers charge the most per event.

04

Carrier allocation optimization

Linear programming model computes the optimal shipment split across your carriers. Shows exactly how many shipments to shift, from whom to whom, and the projected annual savings.

05

Prioritized savings action plan

Top 5 actions ranked by financial impact. Each action names the carrier, the lane, the dollar amount, and the specific step to take. Not "consider renegotiating" — "call ABC Carriers about invoice SHP-12903."

06

30-minute video walkthrough

A recorded walkthrough of your report by a PhD-level operations research analyst. Not a generic overview — a specific conversation about your data, your carriers, your lanes, your savings.

How it works

Three steps. Five business days.

1

Send us your freight data

Export 3-6 months of freight invoices from your TMS or carrier portal. CSV or Excel. We provide a template if needed. A 15-minute discovery call helps us understand your network and priorities.

2

We run the analysis

Our operations research models process your data: linear programming for carrier allocation, IQR for invoice anomalies, activity-based costing for lane profitability, SPC control charts for performance variance. No AI hallucinations — deterministic math on your actual numbers.

3

You get your report + walkthrough

Within 5 business days: a branded PDF report with every finding, every dollar amount, and every action step — plus a 30-minute recorded video walkthrough explaining the results and recommendations.
