BizAnalytic – Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2025
Operator:BizAnalytic, operated by Adil Akaaboune, sole proprietor (trading as “BizAnalytic”)
Contact:support@bizanalytic.com · 77380 · US/Texas

  1. Quick Summary

  • Subscriptions (monthly/annual): Cancellable anytime; fees already paid are non-refundable, except where required by law. We offer account credits for verified service outages (see SLA Credits).
  • One-off reports / pay-per-use analyses: Refundable only until processing starts. Once a report is generated or viewed/downloaded, it’s non-refundable.
  • Professional services (custom setups, data cleaning, bespoke analytics): Require a non-refundable deposit; milestone-based remainder is non-refundable once delivered.
  • How to request: Email support@bizanalytic.com within the applicable window (see below) with your order ID and reason.

  1. Definitions

  • “Subscription”: A recurring plan (monthly or annual) granting platform access/features.
  • “One-off Report”: A single analytical output purchased per file/run (e.g., PDF/CSV).
  • “Professional Services”: Any custom work, onboarding, data mapping, or advisory billed separately.
  • “Processing Start”: When our system begins computing your report or when an analyst starts work on your request.

  1. Free Trials & Previews (if enabled)

If your plan includes a free trial or preview:

  • No charges during the trial. Cancel before it ends to avoid billing.
  • Payments after a free trial are non-refundable (you had an opportunity to evaluate).
    If there is no trial, we recommend purchasing a single low-cost report before upgrading.

  1. Subscriptions (Monthly / Annual)

  • You may cancel anytime from your account settings; access remains until the end of the current paid term.
  • Refunds: Fees already paid are non-refundable (including partial months) except where mandatory law grants a right to withdraw.
  • Downgrades/Upgrades: Prorations are applied to future invoices where applicable; no cash refunds for downgrades mid-term.
  • Auto-renewal: Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date.

Optional “First-Purchase Grace” (use if you want a softer stance):
For a customer’s first-ever subscription, if they cancel within 7 calendar days and have not generated more than 1 report nor uploaded more than 50MB of data, we’ll refund that first charge upon request. (Remove this paragraph if you prefer a stricter policy.)

  1. One-Off Reports / Pay-Per-Use

  • Refundable before processing: If you request a refund before Processing Start, we will refund the fee to the original payment method.
  • Non-refundable after processing/delivery: Once a report is generated or viewed/downloaded, the purchase is final.
  • Corrupt/failed outputs: If a processing failure is on us (system error), we will re-run at no charge or issue an account credit of equal value.

Acceptance & Delivery Proof:
We log generation time, file hash, IP, and download/view events. You may also receive a Delivery Certificate by email. Accessing the report counts as acceptance.

  1. Professional Services & Custom Work

  • Deposit: A 50% non-refundable deposit is due to start.
  • Milestones: Remaining amounts are billed at each milestone and become non-refundable once delivered.
  • Rescheduling/Scope changes: May incur additional fees; deposits remain non-refundable.

  1. Service Availability & SLA Credits (Account Credits, not cash)

We aim for ≥99.5% monthly uptime. If our monthly uptime drops below:

  • 99.5% → 10% credit of that month’s subscription fee
  • 99.0% → 25% credit
  • 95.0% → 100% credit
    Credits apply to future invoices, not to past charges or one-off reports. Planned maintenance and force majeure are excluded.

  1. Data Issues Outside Our Control

Refunds are not provided for:

  • Incorrect, incomplete, or incompatible customer data (e.g., malformed CSVs, wrong column mapping).
  • Third-party outages (carriers, ERPs, mapping APIs) outside our control.
  • User error (accidental deletions, misconfiguration) unless caused by a documented product defect.

We’ll help fix mapping where feasible, and we may offer a complimentary re-run at our discretion.

  1. How to Request a Refund or Credit

Email support@bizanalytic.com with:

  1. Order ID / Invoice number,
  2. Purchase type (subscription / one-off / services),
  3. Reason for request,
  4. Any supporting evidence (screenshots, error IDs).

Windows to request:

  • Subscriptions: within 7 days of billing only if eligible under First-Purchase Grace (if you keep that clause) or mandatory law.
  • One-off reports: before Processing Start.
  • Professional services: prior to the agreed start date (deposit is non-refundable).
    We respond within 2 business days.

  1. Payments, Refund Method & Timelines

  • Payments are processed by our provider (e.g., Stripe).
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method only. Processing times vary by bank and card network (typically 5–10 business days).
  • For bank transfers, refunds are not guaranteed and may be issued as account credits.

  1. Chargebacks & Disputes

  • We take fraud and misuse seriously. Filing a chargeback after delivery may result in account suspension.
  • We provide the payment processor with delivery and acceptance evidence (timestamps, IP logs, download/view records, policy acceptance).
  • To avoid delays, contact us first at support@bizanalytic.com—most issues can be resolved via re-runs or credits.

  1. Abuse & Fair Use

We may refuse refunds and/or terminate accounts that:

  • Repeatedly request refunds after consuming the service,
  • Share or resell deliverables contrary to our Terms,
  • Circumvent usage limits or fair-use policies.

  1. Compliance & Local Laws

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable consumer rights under the laws of your country or the jurisdiction specified in our Terms of Service. Where such rights apply, we will comply with those obligations.

  1. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on our website with a revised “Last updated” date and, where required, notified to active customers. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.
