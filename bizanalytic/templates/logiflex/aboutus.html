About - Adam Akaaboune

PhD in Engineering Science · Professor of Operations & Supply Chain Management · Founder of BizAnalytic

The Woodlands, Texas

Seamless logistics Analysis for Smarter Decisions

I've spent 13 years teaching operations management, supply chain, and business intelligence at the university level. Before that, I spent 8 years in industry — working inside logistics operations, manufacturing plants, and consulting engagements where I saw the same problem over and over again.

Small and mid-size operators are drowning in data they never look at. Their TMS exports sit in folders. Their carrier invoices pile up in spreadsheets. They negotiate rates with gut feel, not evidence. They lose money on lanes they don't know are unprofitable. They keep sending volume to carriers that deliver late 40% of the time. Not because they're bad at their jobs — because nobody gives them the tools to see what's happening.

The enterprise world has Chainalytics, FourKites, project44 — analytics platforms that cost $50K-$200K a year. They're built for shippers moving $10M+ in freight. If you're running a 15-truck fleet or a mid-size brokerage, those tools aren't even aware you exist. You're left with Excel and instinct.

I built BizAnalytic to fix that. Not with a dashboard you'll never open. With reports that tell you exactly what's wrong, exactly what to do about it, and exactly how much money each action saves. The analytics behind every report come from the same operations research models I teach in graduate courses — linear programming for carrier allocation, statistical process control for driver performance, interquartile range detection for invoice anomalies, activity-based costing for lane profitability. The AI writes the narrative. The math produces the numbers. Nothing is estimated or hallucinated.

I'm a sole founder. I wrote every model. I designed every report. When you email support@bizanalytic.com, I'm the one who answers. That's not a limitation — it's a guarantee that the person analyzing your freight data has a PhD in the discipline and has been doing this work for over two decades.

  • Route efficiency and performance

  • Cost breakdowns and savings opportunities

  • On-time delivery analysis

  • Fleet utilization and productivity benchmarks

We go beyond numbers. Each report includes clear recommendations, so you don’t just see how your operations are performing—you understand how to improve them.

Our team brings deep expertise in supply chain management, logistics, and advanced analytics. We combine industry knowledge with the latest in AI technology to deliver tools that help you compete, grow, and thrive in today’s fast-moving logistics environment.

Whether you’re a regional freight carrier, a delivery fleet, or a growing logistics company, our goal is to be your trusted analytics partner. We make decision-making easier, more data-driven, and always aligned with your bottom line.

How I work

Math first. AI second. Always specific.

Every analysis starts with deterministic models — the kind I teach in graduate-level operations research courses. Linear programming computes your optimal carrier allocation. Statistical process control flags drivers whose performance is out of control. Interquartile range detection catches invoice anomalies. Activity-based costing reveals your true lane-by-lane profitability.

The AI layer comes last. After the math has produced every number, an LLM writes the narrative — translating statistical output into plain language a fleet manager can act on without a data science degree. The AI never invents a number. It narrates the ones the models computed.

This matters because it means every dollar amount in your report is traceable to a specific model, a specific calculation, and your specific data. When I tell you "shifting 35% of ABC Carriers volume to XYZ Freight saves $18,240/year," that number comes from a linear program — not from a language model guessing.
