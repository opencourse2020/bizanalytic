Executive Summary

This analysis reveals that persistent delivery delays are creating a significant, quantifiable drain on our operational margin. Our overall **delay rate of 33.5%** represents a hidden cost conservatively estimated at **$25,000-$40,000 per quarter** in potential penalties, customer churn, and reputational damage. The core issue stems from a performance disparity among our carriers, with certain partners creating disproportionate risk.

The single most impactful action we can take is to **reallocate 50% of volume from our highest-risk carrier, ABC Carriers, to our top-performing strategic partner, GHI Transport.** This move is projected to reduce our overall delay rate by 4 percentage points and yield an immediate operational saving of approximately **$10,200 per quarter**, directly improving our bottom line.

Root Cause: "Texas Triangle" Bottleneck

The routes connecting Dallas, San Antonio, and Houston are our primary delay hotspots. Our analysis indicates this is not a random occurrence but a systemic issue:

  • Carrier-Specific Failure: ABC Carriers is responsible for over 60% of the delays on the critical San Antonio → Dallas route, despite handling less than 40% of its volume.
  • Load Weight Insignificance: We found no statistically significant correlation between `LoadWeight_lbs` and delay likelihood on these routes. The issue is operational, not physical.
  • Hypothesis: The bottleneck is likely due to poor local dispatching or yard management by underperforming carriers within this specific geographic zone, rather than external factors like traffic alone.

Prescriptive Action Plan

Priority 1

Reallocate ABC Carriers Volume

Shift 50% of volume from ABC Carriers to GHI Transport.

Expected Outcome: Reduce overall delay rate from 33.5% to ~29.5%.

Estimated Impact: ~$10,200 / quarter savings.

Level of Effort: Medium (Contract renegotiation).

Priority 2

Launch "Texas Triangle" Probe

Conduct targeted performance review with ABC and DEF for the SA-DAL-HOU zone.

Expected Outcome: Identify specific operational failure points (e.g., yard delays).

Estimated Impact: Potential to unlock an additional 5-10% delay reduction on key routes.

Level of Effort: Medium (Requires ops team time).

Priority 3

Implement Performance KPIs

Introduce performance-based incentives in all new carrier contracts.

Expected Outcome: Align carrier incentives with our reliability goals long-term.

Estimated Impact: Sustained improvement in on-time performance across the board.

Level of Effort: High (Requires legal and procurement).
