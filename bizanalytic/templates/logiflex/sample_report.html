Q3 Freight Operations Performance Review

Section 1: Executive Summary

In Q3, Mascaw's freight operations faced significant financial impacts due to delivery delays, with an estimated total cost of $150,000. The most impactful action to address these delays is optimizing carrier selection, particularly by shifting volumes from less reliable carriers to those with better on-time performance. This strategic shift is projected to reduce delay-related costs by 20% and improve overall reliability.

Section 2: Diagnostic Analysis

Root Cause Analysis

The concentration of delays in the "Texas Triangle" (Austin, Houston, Dallas/Fort Worth) is primarily attributed to the inconsistent performance of certain carriers, particularly ABC Carriers, which has a higher delay frequency compared to others. Additionally, analysis shows that delays are more frequent during peak hours and on specific days of the week, indicating potential scheduling inefficiencies.

Carrier Reliability

ABC Carriers has the highest delay rate, while GHI Transport shows better reliability. This suggests a need to reassess carrier partnerships.

Route Distance vs. Delay Frequency

Longer routes tend to have a higher frequency of delays, indicating potential issues with route planning and execution.

Section 3: Carrier 2x2 Matrix

The matrix categorizes carriers based on cost per mile and on-time delivery percentage. Strategic Partners are those with low cost per mile and high on-time delivery, while High-Risk Carriers exhibit high costs and low reliability.

Section 5: Scenario Modeling

Carrier Swap Simulation

Shifting 50% of ABC’s volume to GHI Transport is projected to decrease the average delay rate by 10% and reduce the cost per mile by 5%, resulting in projected savings of approximately $50,000.

Fuel Price Sensitivity

A 10% increase in fuel prices would most severely impact long-distance routes and carriers with higher fuel consumption rates, such as DEF Logistics, potentially eroding margins by up to 8%.
